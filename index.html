Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our yoga programs

Is yoga suitable for beginners?▼

Yes! Our beginner yoga program is specifically designed for people starting yoga for the first time. Swati provides gentle guidance to help you build confidence and proper technique.

Do you teach senior citizens?▼

Absolutely. Our Senior Citizen Yoga program focuses on gentle movement, balance, flexibility, and relaxation. Sessions are adapted to individual comfort and ability.

Do you teach children?▼

Yes, we offer Kids Yoga with fun, age-appropriate activities focusing on movement, balance, flexibility, breathing, and mindfulness.

Do you offer home yoga?▼

Yes, Swati offers personal home yoga sessions — private one-to-one sessions in the comfort of your home.

Do you offer online yoga?▼

Yes, live online personalized yoga sessions are available. Connect with Swati from anywhere with a stable internet connection.

How do I book a class?▼

WhatsApp Swati at +91 82877 34410 or click any "Book on WhatsApp" button on our website to start booking.

How much do classes cost?▼

Pricing depends on the package you choose. Contact us via WhatsApp for current pricing details.
